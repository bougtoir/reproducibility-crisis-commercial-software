# Working journal: EPJ Research Infrastructures

**Preparation only. Recheck the live portal before final submission.**

Official sources:
- https://link.springer.com/journal/41781/submission-guidelines
- https://link.springer.com/journal/41781/aims-and-scope
- https://link.springer.com/journal/41781/how-to-publish-with-us

The independent audit obtained extracted official instructions where direct
requests returned challenges. Research articles are a plausible fit for a
completed infrastructure/access/reconstruction study. The inspected instructions
specify author–year citations and alphabetical references, an abstract of
150–250 words, four to six keywords, inline figures, editable source, and
Statements and Declarations. No verified Registered Report pathway was found.
No general body-word or figure-count limit was identified in inspected content;
that is not evidence that the submission portal has no restrictions.

Figures: preserve editable originals and individual vector/raster files.
The inspected instructions specify at least 1200 dpi for raster line art,
300 dpi for halftones, 600 dpi for combinations, and embedded vector fonts.
Current preparation figure has a vector version; it is conceptual, not empirical.
The general skill suggesting separate figures only applies where the journal
requires that; the preparation review copy uses inline placement.

AI assistance must be disclosed in Methods or an appropriate alternative section;
LLMs cannot be authors and human accountability is required. Provide actual
funding, interests, contributions, affiliations, correspondence, ethics/consent
determination where applicable, and data/code availability. Do not fill author
declarations by inference from the preceding paper.

Audit fee snapshot: £2,490 / US$3,790 / €2,890 plus applicable tax. This is not a
payment commitment; check acceptance-date pricing, institutional agreements and
waiver eligibility. Commercial software purchase policy does not cover journal APCs.

Fallback: Royal Society Open Science. Its official guide uses numbered Vancouver
references and supports Registered Reports, but its AI policy limits replacement
of core researcher tasks. The present autonomous preparation cannot be assumed
compliant merely by calling agents measurement instruments. Human responsibility
and editorial applicability need resolution if that fallback is chosen.

Use, in order: primary numerical endpoint; principal effect; principal performance
metric; central quantitative table; central quantitative figure; principal
computational conclusion. Choose the paper's stated priority, then order of
appearance for equally central candidates. Record alternatives considered and
rejection reasons. A convenient or easy endpoint is never a replacement after
seeing reconstruction output.

Require a computational, central and objectively assessable output, its exact
publication/supplement location, reporting precision, input identity, estimand
and prospective comparison rule. Record the minimal target-linked claim separately.
If a conclusion has no defensible numerical operationalization, G2 is uncertain
or no; do not silently replace the primary endpoint with a subjective judgement.

The manifest records target_id, paper_id, source_id, page/table/figure/cell,
quoted claim, numerical value, units, metric, evaluation population/split,
selection rationale, tolerance, timestamp and selector. Targets and packages
must be hashed and sealed before any reconstruction run. The evaluator's
score specification is fixed; no author implementation is used to choose a target.
Same target and material are delivered to every slot. Do not reveal another
agent's interpretation or an author's implementation in a clarification.

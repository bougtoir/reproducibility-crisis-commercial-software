# Blinded human validation

**NOT COMPLETED. This is a protocol and empty data-entry package, not human data.**

Purpose: assess the validity of the agent measurement process. It does not
estimate human reconstructability in the corpus.

After blind freeze, sample approximately fifteen cases: five complete 3/3,
five mixed (1/3 or 2/3), five complete 0/3. Within group use a frozen seeded
hash order, retaining selection probabilities. If sparse, distribute unfilled
places round-robin across remaining groups in fixed 3/3, mixed, 0/3 order,
without exceeding group size. If fewer cases exist overall, report the reduced
size; do not manufacture cases. Incomplete/contaminated triples are not silently
assigned to an outcome stratum.

The coordinator alone sees selection labels. Give validators anonymous case IDs,
the exact blind publication/input package, target and instructions, without agent
code/logs/outcomes, original implementation or reveal findings. Packages cannot
yet be assembled because no target packages or frozen outcome strata exist.
The release build supplies the data-entry schema and these instructions.

Validator instructions: use the same allowed information, independently implement
the stated method, record observable decisions and errors, and submit code,
execution output, numeric comparison and target-linked conclusion. Record expertise,
time limits, resources, any permitted assistance and deviations. Do not claim
identical time/compute conditions if they are not actually comparable.

Before recruitment obtain the responsible institution's ethics determination
for collecting human performance data, document actual consent arrangements and
privacy/release rules. No exemption, consent, investigator identity or participant
has been presumed. Real human participation must be supplied by the researchers.

Score with frozen rules using an adjudicator masked to agent outcome and sampling
group. Record reviewer disagreements before consensus. Report a paired case table,
discordant cases with evidence, and agreement proportion with its denominator;
any binomial interval is descriptive conditional on the purposive outcome-balanced
sample. Do not extrapolate it to all humans or all papers. Do not simulate responses.
Empty `human_results.csv` remains NOT COMPLETED until actual submissions exist.
